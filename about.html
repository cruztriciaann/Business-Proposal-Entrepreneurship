About TOKI

TOKI is an attendance tracking system designed with one core principle: timing is everything.

Our Mission

TOKI was created to help students and educators build discipline through accurate, automated attendance tracking. Instead of manual roll calls or paper logs, TOKI ensures every record is precise, consistent, and time-based. No more guesswork — just clean, verifiable data.

The Philosophy

The system reflects a simple idea — small moments of time define responsibility, presence, and discipline. When you show up on time, it reflects character. TOKI makes that visible and measurable.

Security by Design

TOKI uses a dual-verification approach: a time-limited verbal code plus GPS geofencing. Both conditions must be met simultaneously. This makes it virtually impossible to check in remotely or have someone else mark attendance on your behalf.
