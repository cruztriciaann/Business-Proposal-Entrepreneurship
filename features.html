Features

Time-Accurate Attendance

TOKI records attendance based on exact timestamps — ensuring every check-in and check-out is precise and verifiable. No rounding, no estimates.

Smart Attendance Records

Automatically organizes attendance logs so students and organizers can easily track presence, absences, and tardiness in one clear view.

Real-Time Verbal Codes

Organizers generate ephemeral 4-digit codes spoken aloud in the venue. Students have a 5-minute window to enter — preventing remote check-ins entirely.

GPS Geofencing

The organizer's physical location is captured when creating the event. Students must be within the configured radius (30–500m) to check in successfully.

Discipline System

Built around the principle: timing is everything. Encourages punctuality and accountability by making tardiness immediately visible.
