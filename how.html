How It Works

Organizer Flow
1

Create an Event

Sign in as an organizer, navigate to Manage Event, and fill in the event details. TOKI captures your GPS position as the venue center for geofencing.

2

Open Check-in Window

When students are physically inside the venue, open the check-in window. A 4-digit code appears — announce it verbally. Students have 5 minutes to enter it.

3

Open Check-out Window

Before the event ends, open the check-out window and announce the new code. Students who are still present can check out, confirming they stayed for the duration.

4

View Records & Export

The Attendance List shows every student's check-in and check-out times. Use Manual Entry for any corrections, and Announcements to post event details.

Student Flow
1

Sign In & Check Dashboard

Log in with your student number and password. Your dashboard shows the active event, your current status, and quick action buttons.

2

Enter Check-in Code

When the organizer announces the verbal code, go to Check-in, enter the 4-digit code, and allow GPS access. TOKI verifies your location and records your arrival time.

3

Check Out Before You Leave

Before leaving, go to Check-out and enter the departure code. This confirms your attendance for the full duration of the event.
