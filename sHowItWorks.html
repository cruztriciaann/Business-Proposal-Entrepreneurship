How It Works
A quick guide to TOKI attendance
Your 3-Step Process
Simple, fast, tamper-proof
1
Arrive at the Venue
Be physically present before the window closes
2
Enter the Verbal Code
Listen for the 4-digit code announced by your organizer
3
Check Out Before Leaving
Enter the departure code to confirm you attended the full event
Important Rules
Do NOT share the code
The verbal code must stay inside the venue. Sharing it counts as attendance fraud.
5-Minute Window
You have exactly 5 minutes after the code is announced to submit it. After that, the window closes.
GPS Verification
TOKI verifies your physical location. You must be within the venue's GPS radius to check in successfully.
Always Check Out
Checking in without checking out marks your attendance as incomplete. Always use the check-out code before you leave.
FAQ
What if I missed the check-in window?
Contact your organizer immediately. They can manually add your check-in from the Manual Entry page if they can verify your presence.
My GPS says I'm outside the venue but I'm inside — what do I do?
GPS accuracy can vary indoors. Try moving closer to a window or open area. If it still fails, ask your organizer for a manual entry.
Can I check in for someone else?
No. Each account is tied to a student number. Checking in for someone else is attendance fraud and may result in disciplinary action.
What does "Complete" attendance mean?
"Complete" means you both checked in AND checked out. If only checked in, your record shows "Inside" — incomplete attendance.
