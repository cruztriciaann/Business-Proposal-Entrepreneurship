Frequently Asked Questions
Can we try TOKI before subscribing?
Yes! The Free Pilot plan lets you run one event with up to 50 attendees at no cost — the best way to experience TOKI before committing.
Is billing monthly or annual?
Subscriptions are billed monthly per institution. No long-term contracts — cancel or change plans any time.
What counts as an "event"?
Any session with its own check-in/check-out window — seminars, orientations, assemblies, sports days, etc.
Can we upgrade or downgrade our plan?
Yes. Switch plans at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
